# 🌱 Garden Glory

**A tiny idle/tower-défense garden game living in a single HTML file.** No installs, no servers — open `index.html` in any browser and your garden is already growing.

Plant, defend, evolve. Beat six worlds of twenty stages each, breed a living bestiary of enchanted bugs, and turn your humble patch into a legendary oasis.

## 🎮 How it plays

- **Plant your loadout** — eco plants earn Sheckles mid-wave, shooters rain damage on bugs, blockers hold the line.
- **Six realms, 120 stages** — clear Stage 20 of a world to unlock the next one, and earn new plants along the way.
- **Daily loop** — daily quests, a daily-reward calendar, streaks, and a rotating festival mood on the Events page.
- **Meta progression** — spend Sheckles in the Laboratory, Gems in the Summoner, and rare Enchanting Shards in the Enchanter.

## ✨ What makes it shiny

- **🔥 Hell Mode** (unlocks after your first full clear) — the Underworld reddens, runes and skeletons creep in, Hell Bosses loom, and Hell Bugs & Fusion Bugs spawn. Bigger rewards, bigger danger.
- **🪄 Enchanter** (unlocks **Sep 26**) — evolve plants with Enchanting Shards. Each evolution resets the plant to Lv 1 but grants **+35% power forever — and a brand-new evolved form**. Enchant once and your carrot grows into a golden triple-barrel spiked cannon.
- **☯ Zen Event** (unlocks **Oct 1**) — a seasonal celebration. Earn Zen Petals in battle and awaken plants to deeper peace. Countdown teaser lives right on the home garden.
- **🎪 Garden Blossom Festival** — the Events page dresses up with bunting, confetti, streak fires, and a **Coming Up** board tracking the next seasonal event.
